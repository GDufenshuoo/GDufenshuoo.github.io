CM1A / CM1A-LBCC
The CM1A charge model originates from AMSOL. The LBCC bond-charge
corrections follow the published CM1A-LBCC model:
L. S. Dodda, J. Z. Vilseck, J. Tirado-Rives, W. L. Jorgensen,
J. Phys. Chem. B 2017, DOI: 10.1021/acs.jpcb.7b00272.

OPLS-AA
OPLS-AA force-field parameters are scientific data published in:
W. L. Jorgensen, D. S. Maxwell, J. Tirado-Rives,
J. Am. Chem. Soc. 1996, 118, 11225.
The public OPLS-AA 2023-10-01 parameter set is used here.

This tool is for research and educational use. Validate generated
parameters and geometries independently before production simulations.
